WHAT IS SPHEREx?

A NASA infrared observatory mapping the entire sky in 102 spectral channels, 0.75–5 microns.

WHAT IS SPHEREx?

SPHEREx launched in March 2025 and surveys the whole sky roughly every 6 months. Each pointing is observed through a linear-variable filter, splitting light into 102 channels. Because the sky is re-observed repeatedly, we can search for things that change position or brightness over time — that repeated coverage is what makes a motion search possible.

WHAT IS THE PLANET X SEARCH?

A hypothetical scientific search concept — not a confirmed discovery.

WHAT IS THE PLANET X SEARCH?

Some models of the outer Solar System suggest an additional planet could exist. No such object has been confirmed. In this platform, "candidates" are simply positions or measurements that a user flagged for follow-up. An evidence score measures how complete the investigation is — it is NOT a discovery probability, and no measurement here is ever labeled Planet X without defensible evidence.

WHAT IS A SPECTRUM?

Light carries fingerprints. Splitting it reveals what an object is made of and how bright it is at each wavelength.

WHAT IS A SPECTRUM?

Every channel of a SPHEREx observation is an image taken at a slightly different infrared wavelength. By measuring the same position in many channels, we build a spectrum — a brightness-versus-wavelength curve. Unusual shapes can be interesting, but interpreting them requires care: instrumental effects, zodiacal light and noise can all mimic features.
